# Data Cleaning Log

### 1. List of Issues Found
* High volumes of trailing text padding and broken character casing across descriptive customer and geographic strings.
* Systematic shorthand typings and misspellings (e.g., "Tehc", "Hmoe Office").
* Corrupted date entries stored as unparseable text format strings.
* Logical chronology breaks where the logged shipping dates occurred before the order timestamps.
* Discrepancies between historical system-generated revenue and calculated mathematical boundaries.

### 2. Cleaning Actions Performed
* Applied a global text parsing layer utilizing `=TRIM(PROPER())` to isolate and strip structural string anomalies.
* Executed data type transformations via Text-to-Columns functions to enforce native serialization on dates.
* Replaced systematic spelling defects across product categorizations with correct enterprise taxonomy values.

### 3. Business Rules Applied
* Implemented strict transactional formulas: `Sales = Quantity * Unit Price * (1 - Discount)`.
* Enforced non-negative boundaries across financial margins.
* Segmented and excluded all rows displaying explicitly failed payment structures from active corporate revenue targets.

### 4. Assumptions Made
* Order items missing critical regional classifications but possessing correct municipal keys were mapped back to their geographic origins.
* Cancelled orders represent zero active revenue contribution and are isolated from baseline growth analytics.

### 5. Records Removed
* **Count:** 0 records deleted. (Per business auditing instructions, row volumes were completely preserved to guarantee macro database integrity; faulty records were dynamically filtered or structurally updated instead).

### 6. Records Flagged
* All rows showcasing `shipping_delay_days < 0` were isolated and tagged as `"Invalid Date Order"` using automated conditional array checking for manual corporate review.

### 7. Limitations of Your Cleaning Process
* The audit process is restricted to flat-file calculations within Excel. Without a direct connection to live transactional master data databases, structural identity discrepancies between matching Order IDs with non-matching customer files cannot be definitely merged.
